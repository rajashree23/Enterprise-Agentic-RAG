# Service ownership

| Service | Owner | Repository | On-call channel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment API | Payment Platform | atlas-payment-api | payment-oncall |
| Fraud Service | Risk Engineering | atlas-fraud | risk-oncall |
| Ledger Service | Finance Platform | atlas-ledger | finance-oncall |
| Provider Adapter | Payment Platform | atlas-provider-adapter | payment-oncall |

# Escalation rule

Payment incidents are initially assigned to Payment Platform. Ledger reconciliation failures are escalated to Finance Platform.
